# Intended Side Effects — V9 Hover Growth

D3.js + SVG, without Canvas. Dots move slowly within their local areas and grow near the cursor, without being displaced.

Start in this folder with `py -m http.server 3000` and open http://localhost:3000.

Change `hoverRadius` and `hoverScale` in `script.js` to adjust the hover effect. `hoverScale: 3.2` means dots at the cursor center are up to 3.2× their usual radius.
